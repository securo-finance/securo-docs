On a brand-new install Securo has no users yet, so opening **http://localhost:3000** takes you straight to the setup screen. This screen only exists while the instance is empty: once the first account is created, it redirects to the regular login page.

## Create Your Admin Account

The setup screen asks for everything in a single form:

- **Your name**: How Securo should greet you
- **Email and password**: Your login credentials for this instance
- **Currency**: Your primary currency, used as the default for the dashboard, budgets, and reports

You can also pick the interface **language** and switch between **light and dark theme** right on this screen.

If you manage money in multiple currencies, don't worry: accounts can each have their own currency, and Securo converts everything to your primary currency for aggregated views.

## What Securo Creates for You

Submitting the form sets up a ready-to-use instance:

- A **personal workspace**, the container that holds all your financial data
- A **Wallet** checking account, so you can record your first transaction immediately
- A set of **default [categories](/docs/features/categories)** covering common spending areas (Housing, Groceries, Transport, Salary & Income, and more), already localized to your language

There is no starting balance to enter and nothing else to configure up front. You can rename or remove any of this later.

## The Guided Tour

After setup you land on the [Dashboard](/docs/features/dashboard) and a short six-step tour points out the essentials: the sidebar navigation, the dashboard itself, accounts, transactions, file import, and categories. You can skip it at any point, it exists purely to orient you.

## After the Tour

From here, we recommend:

1. **Add your real accounts** in [Accounts](/docs/features/accounts), each with its current balance
2. **Add a few transactions** manually, or [import](/docs/features/import) your bank history (OFX, CSV, QIF, and CAMT are supported)
3. **Create categorization rules** in [Rules](/docs/features/rules) so future transactions are sorted automatically
4. **Set up budgets** in [Budgets](/docs/features/budgets) for your main spending categories
5. **Add recurring transactions** in [Recurring](/docs/features/recurring) for subscriptions and regular bills
