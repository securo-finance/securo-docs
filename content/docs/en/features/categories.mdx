Categories are how Securo organizes your spending. Every transaction can be assigned to a category, and those categories power your budgets, reports, and dashboard widgets. Groups are optional: categories work on their own, and you can organize them into **category groups** whenever your list grows.

![Categories](/screenshots/categories.png)

## Default Categories

A fresh install seeds a set of ready-to-use categories covering the common areas, localized to your language: Housing, Food & Dining, Groceries, Transport, Health, Leisure, Subscriptions, Education, Shopping, Personal Care, Donations, Taxes & Fees, Transfers, Investments, Salary & Income, and Other. Rename, recolor, or delete any of them, they're a starting point, not a constraint.

## Category Groups

Groups are the optional top level: create them yourself and move categories inside to organize the list. Each group has its own icon and color and can be expanded or collapsed in the category list.

## Individual Categories

Inside each group, you'll find specific categories. For example, the Food group might contain:

- **Dining**: Restaurants and dining out
- **Groceries**: Grocery stores and supermarkets

Each category has:
- A **name**
- An **icon** (emoji-based)
- A **color** (hex code) for use in charts and visualizations
- An optional **parent group**

Two flags change how a category behaves in the numbers:

- **Treat as transfer**: transactions in this category are excluded from income and expense totals, like real transfers. The default Transfers and Investments categories ship with this on, so moving money to your brokerage never shows up as "spending"
- **Ignored**: the category's transactions stay visible but are left out of totals and reports

## Creating a Category

1. Go to **Categories** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ Add Category**
3. Enter the category name
4. Choose an icon and color
5. Select the parent group
6. Click **Save**

## Creating a Category Group

1. On the Categories page, click **+ Add Group**
2. Enter the group name
3. Choose an icon
4. Click **Save**

New categories can then be added inside this group.

## Editing Categories

Click the pencil icon next to any category or group to edit its name, icon, color, or parent group. You can also reorganize categories by moving them between groups.

Use the **Collapse All** toggle at the top to quickly collapse or expand all groups.

## How Categories Are Used

Categories flow through the entire application:

- **Transactions**: Each transaction is assigned to one category
- **Budgets**: You set spending limits per category
- **Dashboard**: The spending chart breaks down expenses by category
- **Reports**: All analytics are grouped by category
- **Rules**: Automation rules can auto-assign categories based on transaction descriptions

Uncategorized transactions are highlighted on the dashboard so you're always aware of what needs attention.
