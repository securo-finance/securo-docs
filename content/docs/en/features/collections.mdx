Collections group your accounts (and asset wallets) into named sets: Daily, Long-term, Emergency fund, Family, a side project. Once you have a few, you can look at one slice of your finances at a time instead of always seeing everything mixed together.

## How Collections Work

A collection is just a named list with a color. It can contain:

- **Accounts**: any of your bank accounts, cards, or wallets
- **Wallets**: asset groups from the [Assets](/docs/features/assets) page

An account can belong to more than one collection, and deleting a collection never touches the accounts or data inside it.

## Filtering by Collection

Pick a collection in the filter and the **dashboard, reports, and transactions** all scope themselves to it: totals, charts, and lists show only the accounts in that collection until you clear the filter or switch to **All accounts**.

This is the fastest way to answer questions like "how is the emergency fund doing?" or "what did the household accounts spend this month?" without the noise of everything else.

## Managing Collections

1. Open **Collections** from the accounts page (or the sidebar filter)
2. Click **New collection**
3. Name it, pick a color, and tick the accounts and wallets that belong
4. Save

Edit or delete a collection any time from the same place.
