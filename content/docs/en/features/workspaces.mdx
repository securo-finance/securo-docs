---
title: Workspaces
description: Keep separate financial spaces and share them with the people who need access, each with their own role.
---

A workspace holds a complete, independent set of financial data: its own accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, and goals. Your first workspace is created automatically at [first launch](/docs/getting-started/first-launch), and you can create more whenever one pot of data shouldn't mix with another: household finances separate from a side project, or a space you manage for someone else.

## Creating and Switching

The **workspace switcher** in the sidebar shows all your workspaces and lets you jump between them; everything in the app always operates on the active one. Click **New workspace** to create another, each has its own name, icon, type (Personal, Freelancer, Small business, or Accountant firm), default currency, and language.

A workspace you no longer use can be **archived** from its settings page: it disappears from the switcher but all data is preserved.

## Sharing a Workspace

Open **Workspace settings** to invite people by email. If the email isn't registered yet, you can set an initial password and the account is created on the spot, handy for setting someone up in person.

Each member has a role:

| Role | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full access: manage members, settings, and all data |
| **Editor** | Read and write all financial data |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access |

Removing a member revokes their access to the workspace but never deletes their user account.

## Managed Workspaces

A workspace can also be operated by someone who isn't a regular member: an external **manager** with effective owner rights. The workspace shows an "Externally managed" note so it's always clear that arrangement exists. This suits any situation where you want someone you trust to keep the books in order while the workspace remains yours.

## What Stays Separate

Data never leaks between workspaces: accounts, transactions, categories, rules, budgets, goals, bank connections, and reports are all scoped to the active workspace. Your user account, login credentials, and personal preferences are yours across all of them.
